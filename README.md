## Career Goal

- Product Analyst
- Growth Analyst
- Data Analyst
- Business Intelligence Analyst

with a long-term interest in Gaming, Mobile Apps, and Sports Analytics.

---

## Learning Philosophy

I believe analytics is more than writing SQL queries or building dashboards.

For every project I try to answer four questions:

1. What is the business problem?
2. Which data do I need?
3. What insights can I generate?
4. What action would I recommend?
